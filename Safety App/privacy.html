Privacy Policy

Safety Superheroes is built to need as little information about you as possible. Here is exactly what that means.

No accounts, no logins

You never create an account or sign in to use Safety Superheroes. There is no username, password, email address, or profile stored anywhere for using the app.

Everything stays on your device

Your preferences (color theme, text size, language, Calm Mode, sound on/off) and your progress (which lessons you've completed, favorites, game best streaks) are saved only in your browser's local storage, on your own device. None of it is uploaded to a server, and I cannot see it. Clearing your browser's site data for Safety Superheroes will erase it, and using a different device or browser starts fresh.

No ads, no analytics tracking, no selling data

Safety Superheroes has no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking scripts, and no social media pixels. There is nothing to sell, because nothing about you is collected in the first place.

Works offline, by design

Once you've visited the site, a service worker saves the app's files so it keeps working without an internet connection. This is a standard web feature (a Progressive Web App) and does not involve sending any additional information anywhere.

A note for parents and educators

Because Safety Superheroes doesn't collect names, birthdates, contact information, photos, audio, or any other personal information from any user — child or adult — there is nothing to request the deletion of. If you have questions about how this app handles data, or a request related to a specific device, reach out using the contact below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future feature needed to collect information it doesn't today — this page will be updated first, and the change will be described clearly, in plain language.

Questions?

If anything here is unclear, or you have a question about privacy, reach out any time.
